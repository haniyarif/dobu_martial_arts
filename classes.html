Our Classes

Explore our wide range of martial arts and fitness programs. Whether you're a beginner or experienced, young or old — there’s a class for you.

Brazilian Jiu-jitsu

Focuses on grappling and ground control techniques. Suitable for beginners and advanced students. Classes available for kids and adults.

Benefits: Improve your flexibility, stamina, and self-defense skills while learning effective submission techniques.

Class Structure: Warm-up and drills, technique demonstration, partner practice, and controlled sparring sessions.

What to Bring: Comfortable gi or workout clothes, water bottle, and a positive attitude!

Karate

Traditional striking art that teaches discipline, balance, and speed. Classes focus on kata, self-defense, and sparring.

Benefits: Enhances coordination, agility, and mental focus while building confidence and respect.

Class Structure: Basic techniques, kata practice, partner drills, and controlled sparring.

What to Bring: Karate gi or suitable martial arts attire, sparring gear (optional), and water.

Judo

Olympic martial art based on throws and submissions. Great for fitness, competition, and self-defense skills.

Benefits: Builds strength, balance, and technique for efficient throws and groundwork.

Class Structure: Warm-ups, technique demonstration, nage-waza (throwing practice), and randori (free practice).

What to Bring: Judo gi, mouthguard (optional), and water bottle.

Muay Thai

Known as the "Art of Eight Limbs" — powerful striking using fists, elbows, knees, and shins. Intense cardio workout and self-defense system.

Benefits: Improves cardiovascular fitness, power, and mental toughness.

Class Structure: Warm-up, pad work, technique drills, clinching practice, and sparring.

What to Bring: Comfortable training clothes, hand wraps, gloves, and water.

Kids Martial Arts

Safe, fun, and structured classes designed to build focus, confidence, discipline, and fitness for children aged 4–13.

Benefits: Develops motor skills, social interaction, and healthy habits early on.

Class Structure: Games-based warm-ups, basic technique drills, coordination exercises, and group activities.
